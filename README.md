### Kevin Jones

I build software that turns scattered information and risk signals into something people can act on. My focus is Southeast Asia and human security: scam compounds, forced criminality, trafficking, and the information gaps that let them grow.

I'm a full-stack engineer (8+ years in finance, decentralized tech and government), with graduate training in international policy at GW's Elliott School and RSIS in Singapore.

#### Current work

Most of my current code is in private repos. Here's what it powers:

- **[Blackbook](https://blackbook.fyi)**: a weekly intelligence brief for Black residents and visitors in Bangkok. Every item is sourced and verified, and an automated research pipeline drafts items for editorial review. During the September 2026 floods it moved to daily updates. *Next.js, TypeScript, Postgres (Supabase), Prisma, Resend, Vercel.*
- **[Multidimensional Scam Risk Index (MSRI)](https://msri-delta.vercel.app/)**: early-warning risk scores for the structural conditions that let trafficking-driven scam compounds grow. It's built for NGOs, AML teams and policy teams. It started as my GW capstone and is in active development. *Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind.*
- **[The Mekong Club](https://themekongclub.org)**: strategic design for their Supplier Toolkit, a series of briefs on forced labor and labor-rights risk in supply chains.
